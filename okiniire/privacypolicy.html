Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026 / Last updated: July 12, 2026

KAMKAM provides the OkiniIre app as a free app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information Stored by the App

OkiniIre lets you create favorite groups and items, add descriptions, and attach images. This content is stored on your device. KAMKAM does not operate a server that collects the favorite tree content you enter in the app.

Images and Camera Access

The app may ask for access to your photo library or camera when you choose to attach images to an item. Images you select or capture are used to show those attachments inside the app.

Advertising and Data Handled by Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display ads. For ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically send the following information off your device to Google and share it with Google's services or advertising partners:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location
  • App launches, taps, ad impressions, and other user or ad interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information such as app or SDK launch time, hangs, energy usage, and crash logs
  • Device and account-related identifiers, including an advertising identifier and app- or developer-bounded device identifiers

Depending on your region, choices, and device settings, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob privacy.

Regional Privacy Choices

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google's consent message is shown only when the applicable message is configured and published in the AdMob console and Google's UMP determines that it is required. In that case, you can use the in-app “Privacy settings” entry point (shield icon) to review or change your choices and withdraw your consent. Subsequent ad serving follows your updated choices.

In applicable U.S. states, when the applicable message is configured and published in the AdMob console and Google's UMP determines that it applies, you can use the same “Privacy settings” entry point to opt out of personalized advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information as those terms are defined by applicable law. The options shown vary by region and by the messages configured with Google.

Your operating system may also let you reset, delete, or limit use of the advertising identifier. The choices described here do not limit any rights available under applicable law.

Cookies

OkiniIre does not use browser cookies directly. Third-party services used by the app may use similar technologies according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. KAMKAM does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Security

KAMKAM values your trust and takes reasonable care with information related to the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
