Perfect Pull!

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026

KAMKAM provides Perfect Pull! as a free app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use it.

What the app stores

For each difficulty, the app stores your play record on your device: the scores of your recent successful pulls, how many times you attempted, how many times you succeeded, and your best score so far.

This stays on your device. KAMKAM does not run a server that collects play records, and the app does not ask you to create an account. Deleting the app deletes the records with it.

The app does not request access to your photos, camera, contacts, location or microphone.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertising. To serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent abuse, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically sends the following off your device to Google, and may share it with Google services or advertising partners.

  • IP address (which may be used to infer the approximate region of the device)
  • Usage information such as app launches, taps, and ad impressions and interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information such as app and SDK start times, hangs, energy use and crash logs
  • Advertising identifiers and device or account identifiers scoped to the app or developer

Depending on your region, your consent choices and your device settings, the ads shown may be personalised, non-personalised or limited. For how Google handles this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the AdMob privacy information.

App Tracking Transparency

The app does not request permission through iOS App Tracking Transparency. You will not see a prompt asking to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Regional privacy choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message is shown when the app starts if Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) determines that one is required. Your choice is applied to the advertising you are served from then on.

The app does not include a screen for revisiting that choice later. To make the choice again, delete the app and install it again. Your device settings may also let you reset, delete or limit the use of the advertising identifier. Nothing in this policy limits the legal rights you have in your region.

Cookies

The app does not use browser cookies directly. Third-party services used by the app may use similar technologies under their own policies.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. KAMKAM does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Security

KAMKAM values your trust and takes reasonable care with information related to the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions or suggestions about this policy, contact info@kamkam.me.
