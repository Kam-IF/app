A one-shot physics game for iPhone

One pull. Keep every plate.

The classic tablecloth trick, in your pocket. There is nothing to learn — just flick your finger down the screen. Hesitate and the plates come with it. Pull sloppily and they topple.

Based on what the app actually does

How fast you pull is the whole game.

3D

Real physics, dish by dish

Friction, momentum, how high the centre of gravity sits, how a stack leans. It behaves the way it would on a real table, so there is no trick beyond pulling fast and clean.

%

Not all wins are equal

Every success is scored on how little the dishes moved. A plate that survived by a whisker and a pull so clean nothing shifted are not the same thing.

4

A new table every time

Four difficulties change how many dishes there are, what shapes they take and how they sit. Tables are built fresh each time, so the same layout never comes back.

Rec

Your record pulls you back

Each difficulty keeps your recent average and clear rate. The average only looks at recent successes, so one lucky pull will not carry you.

Grading

Clearing it stops being enough.

The closer you get to the top, the more your finger speed shows up in the number. Beat your personal best and the game tells you right away.

  • Perfect Barely anything moved
  • Great Clean, never in danger
  • Good It survived, but it travelled
