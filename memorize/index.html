Remember the order. Train your focus.

Watch the numbers appear, then tap them back in the same order after they disappear. Memorize is a simple memory game built for short, focused challenges.

6 Difficulty modes

How It Works

Easy to start. Hard to master.

The game begins with fewer numbers. As your level rises, the display speed gets faster, turning a tiny break into a sharp memory challenge.

01

Watch the numbers

Numbers appear one after another. Keep their order in your head.

02

Tap them back

When the display ends, tap the numbers in the order you remember.

03

Level up

Each difficulty has levels 1 to 43. The higher you go, the faster the game becomes.
