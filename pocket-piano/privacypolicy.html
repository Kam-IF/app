Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

Overview

KAMKAM provides PocketPiano as a free app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and this website.

PocketPiano is designed as a simple playable piano keyboard. The app does not require an account, and KAMKAM does not ask you to submit personal information to use the app.

Information the App Handles

The app uses local audio assets and app settings to provide piano playback, note labels, synth controls, and privacy options. These settings are used to operate the app experience.

KAMKAM does not directly collect names, email addresses, music performances, recordings, or account information through PocketPiano.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

PocketPiano uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob to display ads. These third-party services may collect device information, identifiers, usage data, and consent status in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Information

Where required, the app may show a consent form or privacy options form provided through Google's User Messaging Platform.

Website Analytics and Ads

This website may load Google Tag Manager and Google advertising scripts. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic, improve delivery, and show ads.

Children's Privacy

PocketPiano is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. KAMKAM does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Security

KAMKAM values your trust and uses reasonable care in operating the app and website. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Links to Other Sites

The app or website may link to external sites such as the App Store or Google policy pages. KAMKAM is not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of third-party sites.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
